Support

Write to admin@etducky.com. One person reads and answers this mailbox, usually within one business day.

Before you write

Pairing code
Codes work once and expire after 15 minutes. Make a new one in the parent portal under Devices.
Sign-in link
Links work once and expire after 15 minutes. Ask for another from the sign-in page; check the spam folder for mail from hello@ducklingtablet.com.
A device shows "This device was released"
The family account was deleted or the device was removed from the portal. Pair it again with a new code to bring it back.
Narrator voice sounds different
When the Duckling narrator is unavailable or the daily voice allowance is used up, the device's own voice reads the lines instead. Nothing is lost.
Remove Duckling from a device
In the parent portal, open the device and choose Release. On the tablet, the parent area behind the PIN has the same control. Either way the lockdown is lifted before anything is removed.
Forgot the parent PIN
Reset it from the parent portal under Settings; the device picks up the new PIN at its next check-in.

What to include

  • The email address you sign in with (never your sign-in link or PIN)
  • The device: Android tablet, iPad or Windows, and its name in the portal
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead

Cancelling

Cancel in the store you subscribed through (Google Play or the App Store) or, for Windows, in the parent portal under Subscription. Access continues to the end of the paid period. To delete the family account and all its data, use Delete account in the portal; every device is released first.
