Duckling Tablet is on Google Play and the App Store

The Duckling Tablet app is published for Android tablets and iPads, with the Windows installer on this site. What the app does, what the parent portal controls, and what the first week looks like.

Duckling Tablet is now available for Android tablets on Google Play and for iPads on the App Store. The Windows version is a signed installer from the download page. All three use the same family account, the same parent portal, and the same subscription.

What the app does

Duckling Tablet turns an Android tablet, an iPad, or a Windows device into a learning device for one child. The app becomes the child's home screen and runs a library of learning apps made by ET Ducky, with no ads and nothing to buy inside an app. The apps cover reading, math, science, logic, art, and music. Most of them have levels from pre-K to 8th grade, and a narrator reads every instruction aloud.

A parent sets the child's grade for each subject. Every app opens at that grade and moves up or down as the child gets things right or wrong. The child's avatar, stars, and progress follow her to every tablet in the family.

How the lockdown works on each platform

On an Android tablet, Duckling becomes the home screen and pins itself in place. The parent sets a screen lock the child does not know, and from then on the Home button, the Back button, and the recent-apps screen lead back to Duckling. On an iPad, the app uses Apple's Screen Time controls to shield other apps and websites, and Guided Access keeps the child inside Duckling. On Windows, the installer adds a child account that opens only Duckling.

The parent area on every platform walks through the steps for that platform and checks each one off as it is done. Removing Duckling lifts the lockdown and clears the apps and data.

What the parent portal controls

The parent portal at parents.ducklingtablet.com works on any phone or computer. From it a parent installs or removes apps on each tablet, chooses which websites open, and sets each child's starting levels. The portal also allows apps installed outside Duckling for a set number of minutes a day, and it invites a second parent. Changes reach a tablet within seconds while it is on and online.

The portal is also where a parent asks for a new app. Describe the app in a few sentences, and the ET Ducky team builds it for every family at no extra cost.

The first week

Install the app and type a parent's email address. A six-digit code arrives by email, and typing it on the tablet creates the family account and starts a 7-day free trial, with no card required. The app then asks for the child's first name and age, sets a parent PIN, and walks through the lockdown steps.

After the trial, the subscription is $5 a month for the whole family, on every platform, billed through Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPad, and a card payment on the web for Windows. One subscription covers every tablet in the family. Cancelling in the store you subscribed through stops the renewal, and access continues to the end of the paid period.

What is next

New apps join the library for every family as they are built, and the parent portal lists each one with a description of what it trains and how it grows with the child. Requests from parents set the order in which they are built. Write to admin@etducky.com with questions about setup, or read the support page for the answers to the common ones.
