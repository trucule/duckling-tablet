Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026. This is the pre-launch version of the policy; the date changes when the policy does.

Duckling Tablet is made and run by ET Ducky LLC ("we"), Bellingham, Washington, United States. This policy explains what information the Duckling Tablet apps, the parent portal at parents.ducklingtablet.com and this website collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and how you can see or delete it. The children's privacy notice is the short version written for parents about the child's data.

The short version

  • We collect what the service needs to work and nothing for advertising. There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no trackers and no third-party analytics in the apps, the portal or this site.
  • About a child we hold a first name, an age (kept as a month and year of birth so it stays right as the child grows), a level per subject, a theme, the stars earned in each app, the avatar the child designs, including its portrait picture, and the home screen background if the child picks one. We do not ask for the day of birth, a photo of the child, a last name, a location or a gender.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.
  • Microphone and camera data are processed on the device. The only exception is a picture the child picks as her home screen background, which may be a photo from Camera Fun; it is stored privately with her profile, and you can remove it at any time.
  • You can delete the family account and every record under it from the parent portal at any time.

Information we collect

From the parent

The email address you sign in with, the time of each sign-in, and the settings you choose: child profiles, device names, allowed websites, time limits, travel mode, hidden apps and app requests. If you attach a file to an app request (a drawing or a photo of a worksheet, for example) we store that file for the request.

About the child

First name, age (kept as a month and year of birth), a level per subject and a theme (colors and interests). The stars earned in each app, the avatar design and its portrait picture are kept on the server so they follow the child to any tablet in the family and show in the parent portal. So is the home screen background, if the child picks one: a painting from Paint Studio or Draw With Me, or a photo from Camera Fun, which may show the child. It is private to the family, shown only on the child's tablets, and deleted when it is removed in the tablet's parent area, when another picture replaces it, or with the child or the account. Progress inside each app stays on the device in the app's own storage and is not sent to us.

About each device

Platform (Android, iPad or Windows), the name you gave it, app version, battery level, last check-in time, which Duckling apps are installed, and a push notification token so the device can be told to update its settings. The device is identified by a random token created at pairing, not by a hardware identifier.

Narrator voice

When an app asks for a line to be read aloud, the text of that line is sent to our server, which has it spoken by Microsoft Azure Speech and keeps the resulting audio clip so that every device can reuse it. Lines that contain the child's name are marked private by the app: they are spoken and returned but never stored. We count the number of characters each device has spoken per day to cap the cost.

AI features

Story Maker and Draw With Me can ask our server to write a story scene or a drawing lesson. The request contains the choices made in the app (characters, place, story kind, reading level, or the name of the thing to draw) and is forwarded to Anthropic's API. It does not contain the child's name or any account identifier.

Billing

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play, the App Store and Stripe. We receive the subscription's status, its end date and a purchase identifier. We never see card numbers. Google, Apple and Stripe handle payment under their own privacy policies.

Technical logs

Our server logs each request's path, result and timing for 14 days to keep the service running. Logs do not contain sign-in tokens, PINs, child names or the content of emails.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, keep devices in step with your settings, deliver apps and the narrator voice, and process app requests.
  • To bill the subscription and know which family is entitled to the service.
  • To answer support requests you send us.
  • To keep the service secure, including an audit trail of changes made to a family account.

We do not use personal information to build profiles, to advertise, or to train any model.

Your choices

  • See and change everything we hold about your family in the parent portal.
  • Delete the family account and all its data with one action in the portal. Every device is released first, then every record under the family is deleted.
  • Release a single device from the portal, which removes the lockdown and the device record.
  • Write to admin@etducky.com for a copy of your data or any other request. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Sign-in uses one-time email links; we store only a hash of each token. Each family's records are isolated in the database with row-level security, so a request for one family cannot read another family's data. Secrets are kept outside the code and never logged.

Children

Duckling Tablet is used by children under 13 with their parent's involvement; the parent creates the account, consents to the collection described here when starting the subscription, and controls everything about the child's profile and devices. Details are in the children's privacy notice.

Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, we email the account address before the change takes effect and update the date at the top.
