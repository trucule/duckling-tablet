The app library

Twenty-three apps at launch. Every one is made by the same developer, works offline once installed, and has an automated test that runs it end to end before it is published. Grades are approximate: most apps start near 2nd grade and move up or down to meet the child.

  • Across subjects
  • Reading and writing
  • Math
  • Science
  • Thinking
  • Music and voice
  • Art and camera

Across subjects

  • Level Up

    Six subjects, each a ladder of levels: reading, math, color science, patterns, time and money, shapes and fractions. Four right answers in a row move up a level and two misses move down, so it finds the child's level in a few minutes. A wrong answer shows the right one and why. Daily Quest mixes ten questions across subjects.

    K to 4th grade

Reading and writing

  • Trace With Me

    Handwriting in three modes. Little: capital and small letters, numbers and shapes, colored in with one finger. Spell: a spoken word with a picture clue, traced letter by letter in print and then written from memory; the app reads each letter box. Cursive: lowercase letters, joins and words, with a pen that draws each one first.

    Pre-K to 3rd grade

  • Word Wizard

    The child hears a word, sees a picture and taps letter tiles into the slots. The right word casts a spell in a 3D world: rain makes it rain and giant frog makes a giant frog. Levels go from three-letter words to two-word spells, word endings, prefixes and compound words. 292 spells.

    K to 2nd grade

  • Read It Say It Make It

    The child reads a sentence out loud and each word turns green as it is said, then the sentence plays out as an animated 3D scene. Sentences grow from three words to long ones with two animals, weather and first-then order. Speech is checked on the device; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

    K to 2nd gradeMicrophone

  • Story Maker

    Pick one to three friends from a cast of twelve, a place, a story kind, how much trouble to expect and a length. The story is told scene by scene with a choice at the end of each. Three reading levels. At the higher two, each word lights up as it is read, a tapped word is said aloud, and the story ends with comprehension questions.

    Pre-K to 3rd gradeDuckling narrator and AI

Math

  • Count With Me

    Four hands-on math games with their own level ladders. How many: counting, ten frames and arrays. Give me: drag that many into a basket, equal groups, sharing with leftovers. Build it: place value blocks up to three digits with trading. Story: word problems read aloud with a number pad.

    K to 3rd grade

  • Fraction Pizza

    Cut pizzas and pies and serve slices to customers. Levels cover halves and quarters, what is left, equal fractions, adding slices, fractions on a number line and comparing.

    2nd to 4th grade

  • Number Line Rocket

    Tap a spot on the number line and the rocket flies there. The line grows from 0 to 10 up to 0 to 1000, then fractions and numbers below zero. Closer guesses earn more stars.

    K to 4th grade

  • Tangram City

    Drag, turn and flip the seven tangram pieces to fill city shapes. Later levels hide the inside lines, ask for mirror designs, then count squares for area and fence sides for perimeter.

    K to 3rd grade

  • Pattern Machine

    Glowing machines turn, flip, paint and resize block shapes. The child predicts the output, then works out a hidden rule from examples, then follows chains of two and three machines.

    K to 3rd grade

Science

  • Circuit Lab

    Drag batteries, wires, bulbs, switches and buzzers onto a board to light the rooms of a 3D house. A real circuit solver decides brightness, so bulbs in series glow dim and bulbs in parallel glow bright. A short circuit sparks.

    2nd to 4th grade

  • Ecosystem Aquarium

    Set fish, snails, plants, light and food, then run the days. A step by step simulation covers oxygen, food, algae, room to swim and a food chain. A crashed tank names the day and the reason.

    2nd to 4th grade

  • Planet Explorer

    Fly to a planet in a 3D solar system, land and jump. Jump heights, day and year lengths, sizes and tilts come from the NASA Planetary Fact Sheet.

    1st to 4th grade

  • Chemistry Lab

    Safe experiments followed step by step. Each step is read aloud as the child reaches it, and finished experiments earn a star in the book.

    K to 3rd grade

  • Kitchen Lab

    Recipes as experiments: measuring, mixing and watching what changes. Steps are read aloud, and the book keeps a Today card and stars for finished recipes.

    K to 3rd grade

  • Playground Lab

    A 3D physics playground: a slingshot, a catapult, a seesaw, a trampoline, a slide into bowling pins and a row of dominoes. Drop in beach balls, crates, balloons and feathers. Knobs for moon gravity, slow motion and wind.

    Any age

Thinking

  • Robot Path

    Tap arrow cards to program a toy robot to collect gems on a 3D board. Later levels add repeat cards, an if card, card limits and fewest-moves challenges. Every board is checked to be solvable before it is shown.

    K to 3rd grade

  • Light Bridge

    Drag between matching glowing orbs to fill the board without crossing bridges. Boards grow from 4 by 4 to 9 by 9, and each one is proven solvable before it appears.

    K to 3rd grade

Music and voice

  • Simon Orchestra

    A drum, marimba, trumpet, harp, piano and chimes play a tune with spotlights on a 3D stage, and the child plays it back. Tunes grow longer, then faster, then must be played backwards.

    Pre-K to 2nd grade

  • Music Box

    A 16-step tone matrix with three scales, drum, clap and shaker rows, five instruments and the child's own recorded voice as a sixth. Three song slots. The Voice Lab records short sounds onto pads and plays them with effects or up the scale on an animal keyboard.

    Pre-K and upMicrophone

  • Voice Band

    Three rooms built on the child's voice. Sing a Melody turns singing into notes and plays them back on piano, flute, robot, bells or guitar. Voice Effects has ten effects on one recording. Looper Band layers four tracks over a two-bar loop.

    K and upMicrophone

Art and camera

  • Draw With Me

    Step by step drawing lessons, 152 of them from simple to detailed, with a drawing of the day and a surprise pick. Saved pictures earn stars on the lesson tiles. A parent can ask for a drawing that is not in the library yet, and the app learns it.

    Pre-K and upDuckling AI for new drawings

  • Camera Fun

    A camera with filters, face stickers and stamps, a timer, a four-shot photo strip and a say-cheese shutter. Pictures stay on the tablet, in the app's own gallery, where the child can look through and delete them.

    Any ageCamera

Requests

If the app your child needs is not here, describe it in the parent portal. Requests become tickets for the developer, you can follow each one's status, and a finished app joins the Store for every family. When you ask for an app you can tick a box to have it install itself on your devices when it ships.
