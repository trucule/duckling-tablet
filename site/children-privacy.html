Children's privacy notice

Last updated November 15, 2026. For parents, under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Duckling Tablet is made for children under 13 and is set up and controlled by a parent. This notice says exactly what we collect from or about a child, how we use it, and your rights. The full privacy policy covers everything else.

What we collect about a child

  • A first name, entered by the parent, shown on the child's home screen and spoken by the narrator on the device.
  • An age, entered by the parent and kept as a month and year of birth, and a level per subject, so apps begin at the right place.
  • The stars earned in each app and the avatar the child designs, including its portrait picture, so they follow the child to any tablet in the family.
  • The home screen background, if the child picks one: a painting from Paint Studio or Draw With Me, or a photo taken in Camera Fun, which may show the child. It is stored privately with the child's profile, shown only on the child's tablets, and deleted when you remove it in the tablet's parent area, when another picture replaces it, or when you delete the child or the account.
  • A theme (colors and interests) chosen by the parent or the child.
  • Which apps are installed on each device, and a daily count of narrator characters per device.

We do not ask for a last name, the day of birth, a photo of the child, location, gender, school, contact information or any identifier from the child. Progress inside apps stays on the device.

What stays on the device

Microphone audio (Voice Band, Music Box and Read It Say It Make It) and camera images (Camera Fun) are processed on the tablet and not sent to us or to anyone else, except a photo the child picks as the home screen background (above). Speech recognition in Read It Say It Make It runs on the device.

Narrator lines and AI features

Text that an app wants read aloud is sent to our server and spoken by Microsoft Azure Speech. Lines that contain the child's name are never stored. Story Maker and Draw With Me send the choices made in the app to Anthropic's API through our server, without the child's name or any account identifier. Neither provider is allowed to use this data for anything other than producing the response.

Consent

The parent creates the family account with an email address, starts the subscription through Google Play, the App Store or a card payment, and enters the child's profile. Starting the subscription is the parent's consent to the collection described here. We ask for separate consent before any new sharing that is not part of running the service.

Your rights as a parent

  • See everything we hold about your child in the parent portal.
  • Change or remove the first name, age, levels, theme and avatar at any time.
  • Delete the child's profile, or the whole family account and every record under it. Every device is released first.
  • Refuse further collection by releasing the devices and cancelling the subscription; the account is deleted 30 days later.

To exercise any of these by email, write to admin@etducky.com from the address you sign in with.
